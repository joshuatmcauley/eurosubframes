New Audi A4 A5 B8 2 Wheel Drive 2008-2016 Rear Subframe.

This new rear subframe is designed for Audi A4 and A5 B8 2-wheel drive models from 2008 to 2016. It corresponds to the original equipment (OE) part numbers 8K0 505 235 with various suffixes (AA, AL, M, P, S, J, B, E, F, G), each representing different revisions or updates of the same rear axle cross-member.
